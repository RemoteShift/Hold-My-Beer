Bottle Results Screen
=====================

Instant preview
  Unity menu: Hold My Beer > Results > Preview Results (Ctrl+Shift+R).
  This enters Play Mode and immediately opens the presentation with sample results.
  Or enter Play Mode normally and press F8 in the Game view.
  Press any button during counting to speed the sequence up six times.
  Once the stamp has settled, a fresh press dismisses it.
  Preview restores the previous screen and game state. F8 can replay it.
  Stop Play Mode to edit permanent settings.

Settings
  Select the Bottle Results Screen object in Level 1, or its prefab under
  Assets/_Game/UI/Results/Bottle Results Screen.prefab.
  Art references, preview results, timing, tilt, rocking, zoom and grade thresholds
  are exposed in the Inspector. Grade A/B/C require BOTH their time and damage
  thresholds. D is the fallback.

Victory integration
  The scene instance listens to the real Level Manager, not Tutorial Level Manager.
  It freezes RunStats before taking a snapshot. After dismissal it fades to black
  and raises On Finished once. Connect the future next-level sequence there.
  No next scene or extra gameplay has been invented.

Stat definitions
  Time: active game time since the real level started (tutorial excluded).
  Beers Held: successful beer hand-offs, matching the existing EnemiesBeered stat.
  Cocktails Mixed: successfully consumed drinks or launched throws; failed and
    cancelled crafts are excluded.
  Bruises Earned: hits that actually remove health; invulnerability and healing
    do not count. Damage Taken separately sums the actual health removed for grading.
  Trips to Tap: successful tap/barrel refills through BeerHandler.OnRefilled.

Rendering
  The presentation is built by BottleResultsScreen at first use in a distant stage.
  The 977x1213 empty label, NotJamOldStyle14 numbers (42pt, 24px cap height) and
  supplied stamp are rendered together into the bottle's existing curved label UVs.
  Number ink is sampled from the final example: RGB(30,0,59), #1E003B.
  Stamp checkbox centre is aligned to A/B/C/D at 18% above its native size.
  The background and bottle movement use unscaled time while gameplay is paused.
  Camera output follows Game view resolution without stretching.
